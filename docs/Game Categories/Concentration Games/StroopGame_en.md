# 🧠 Stroop Test Game - Player Guide

## 🎯 Objective

The **Stroop Test** is a classic psychological challenge designed to test your **focus** and **mental agility**.

Your goal is to **select the color in which a word is displayed**, _not the word itself_.

For example:

- The word is `BLUE`, but it's painted in **red**.
- You must select: **Red** ✅

---

## 🕹️ How to Play

1. A color word (like `"GREEN"`) will appear on screen.
2. The **ink color** of the word might be different from its **text**.
3. Your task:
   > **Tap the color you see, not the color you read.**

4. A set of color options is displayed below — choose the one that matches the ink.

---

## ⏱️ Time & Difficulty Levels

You have a **limited amount of time** to score as many points as possible.

Each difficulty level gives you different time limits:

| Difficulty | Time Limit |
|------------|------------|
| 🟢 Easy     | 45 seconds |
| 🔵 Medium   | 30 seconds |
| 🔴 Hard     | 15 seconds |

You can track time with a progress bar and countdown timer on screen.

---

## 🧠 Scoring System

- ✅ **+100 points** for each correct answer.
- ❌ **–20 points** for each incorrect answer.
- The game ends when time runs out.

Your total points (🧠 _Neurons_) accumulate in your profile and unlock new content.

## 💡 Pro Tips

- Focus on **what you see**, not what you read.
- Don’t rush — accuracy is more important than speed.
- Try playing in a quiet environment.
- Practice improves your reaction and focus over time.
