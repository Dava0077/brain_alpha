# 🧠 Word Search - Player Guide

## 🎯 Objective

**Word Search** is a visual perception and logic game where your goal is to find hidden words within a scrambled letter grid.

Your objective is to **locate and select the listed target words** by swiping across the correct sequence of letters.

For example:

- If you see `C A T` in a line, tap the letters in sequence to highlight the word **CAT** ✅

---

## 🕹️ How to Play

1. A grid of random letters will appear.
2. Below or above, you’ll see a **list of words to find**.
3. Your task:
   > **Select adjacent letters that form one of the listed words.**

4. Words may appear:
   - ↔️ Horizontally
   - ↕️ Vertically
   - ➚ Diagonally

5. When you select a word correctly, it gets crossed off the list.

---

## ⏱️ Time & Difficulty Levels

You have a **limited amount of time** to find all the words.

| Difficulty | Time Limit |
|------------|-------------|
| 🟢 Easy     | 90 seconds  |
| 🔵 Medium   | 120 seconds |
| 🔴 Hard     | 150 seconds |

A countdown timer will display in MM:SS format during gameplay.

---

## 🧠 Scoring System

- ✅ **+10 base points** for finishing within the optimal time.
- ⚡ Bonus points for faster completion depending on difficulty.
- ❌ No penalty for incorrect attempts, but you lose time.

Your total points (🧠 _Neurons_) accumulate in your profile and unlock more content.

## 💡 Pro Tips

- Scan the grid for repeating patterns or first letters of words.
- Only mark words you're confident in — wrong guesses waste time.
- Practice improves your speed, attention, and visual memory.
- Start with shorter words to gain momentum.
