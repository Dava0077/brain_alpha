# 🧠 Sudoku - Player Guide

## 🎯 Objective

**Sudoku** is a classic logic game designed to train your **concentration**, **logical thinking**, and **problem-solving skills**.

Your goal is to **fill the 9x9 grid** with numbers from 1 to 9, ensuring that:

- Each row contains the digits 1 to 9 without repetition.
- Each column contains the digits 1 to 9 without repetition.
- Each of the nine 3x3 subgrids also contains the digits 1 to 9 without repetition.

---

## 🕹️ How to Play

1. You’ll be shown a partially completed Sudoku grid.
2. Some cells are fixed (gray) and cannot be changed.
3. Tap an empty cell to select it.
4. Choose a number from 1 to 9 to fill the selected cell.
5. You can delete a number if you make a mistake.

> 🧠 Tip: There’s no guessing — just pure logic.

---

## ⏱️ Time & Difficulty Levels

You have a **limited amount of time** to complete the board based on your selected difficulty:

| Difficulty | Time Limit |
|------------|-------------|
| 🟢 Easy     | 10 minutes  |
| 🔵 Medium   | 15 minutes  |
| 🔴 Hard     | 20 minutes  |

A timer will be shown on screen so you can track your progress.

---

## 🧠 Scoring System

- ✅ **Correct Sudoku completed in time**: earn **Neurons** based on difficulty and speed.
- ❌ **Incorrect or incomplete Sudoku**: no points awarded.

Score breakdown by difficulty:

| Difficulty | Optimal Time | Max Neurons |
|------------|----------------|--------------|
| Easy       | ≤ 2.5 minutes   | +10          |
| Medium     | ≤ 4 minutes     | +20          |
| Hard       | ≤ 6 minutes     | +30          |

Neurons are added to your profile and unlock harder levels.

---

## 🔓 Unlocking Difficulties

| Requirement     | Difficulty |
|------------------|-------------|
| None             | 🟢 Easy     |
| 50 Neurons       | 🔵 Medium   |
| 150 Neurons      | 🔴 Hard     |

Higher difficulties provide fewer starting clues and more challenge.

- 🔙 **Back**: Return to the main menu.

## 💡 Pro Tips

- Start with the most obvious numbers or unique placements.
- Use delete if you notice any contradictions.
- Play in a quiet setting for better focus.
- Practice sharpens your logic and speed over time.

Complete the puzzle correctly before time runs out and aim for the **highest score** possible!
