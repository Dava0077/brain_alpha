# 🧠 Pairs Game - Player Guide

## 🎯 Objective
The **Memory Match** game tests your **concentration** and **visual memory**.

Your goal is to **find and match all pairs of cards** in the shortest time possible.

For example:
- Tap on two hidden cards.
- If they match (e.g., both are `A`), they stay revealed ✅
- If not, they flip back over ❌

Match all pairs before time runs out!

---

## 🕹️ How to Play
1. Choose a difficulty level (🟢 Easy, 🔵 Medium, 🔴 Hard).
2. A grid of **face-down cards** appears.
3. Tap on two cards to reveal them.
4. If the cards match, they stay revealed.
5. If they don’t match, they flip back after a second.
6. Continue until all pairs are matched — or time runs out.

> 🧠 Stay focused and remember positions!

---

## ⏱️ Time Limit
You have **90 seconds** to complete the game.

The timer starts when you begin, and ends when:
- All pairs are matched ✅  
- Time runs out ⏳

A timer and score are shown on screen.

## 🧠 Scoring System

Points depend on **difficulty** and **completion time**:

| Time (sec) | Easy | Medium | Hard |
|------------|------|--------|------|
| ≤ 30       | 10   | –      | –    |
| ≤ 45       | –    | 10     | –    |
| ≤ 60       | 7    | 7      | 10   |
| ≤ 75       | –    | 5      | 7    |
| ≤ 90       | 5    | –      | 5    |
| > max      | 3    | 3      | 3    |

Your final score is multiplied by:
- ×1 for 🟢 Easy  
- ×2 for 🔵 Medium  
- ×3 for 🔴 Hard
