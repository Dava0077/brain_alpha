# 🔐 Login Screen - User Guide

## 🎯 Objective

The **Login Screen** is your gateway to accessing the full functionality of the app. It allows users to sign in, register, or continue as a guest.  
Logging in is essential to save your progress, accumulate points (_neurons_), and unlock advanced content.

---

## 🕹️ How It Works

1. Upon launching the app, you are presented with multiple options:
   - Sign in with Google
   - Create a new account (email + password)
   - Log in with existing credentials
   - Continue as a guest (limited access)

2. When signing in:
   - A form appears for email and password input.
   - If credentials are correct, you’ll be taken to your profile.
   - Signing in with Google initiates Google OAuth authentication.

3. Choosing guest mode:
   - You can play without registration.
   - Progress and scores will not be saved.

---

## 🧾 Available Options

| Button                   | Function                                   |
|--------------------------|--------------------------------------------|
| ✨ Continue as Guest      | Enter the main menu without logging in     |
| 🧾 Log In                | Open login form for registered users       |
| 🆕 Create Account         | Open form to create a new account          |
| 🔐 Sign in with Google    | Use your Google account for fast login     |
| ⚙️ Settings               | Change language, sign out, etc.            |

---

## 👥 User Types

- 🧑‍💼 **Registered:** Full access to progress tracking and difficulty unlocks.
- 🧪 **Guest:** Play freely with temporary session data.

---

## 🎨 Interface

- 🖼️ **Header:** Logo and settings access
- 📋 **Main Area:** Login-related buttons and forms
- 🧾 **Status Messages:** Error and success feedback

---

## 🔐 Security

- Passwords are securely handled via Supabase.
- Google Sign-In uses secure OAuth 2.0 flow.
- Input validation is enforced (e.g., empty fields, invalid email).

---

## 💡 Tips

- Register to store your neurons and unlock higher difficulties.
- Use Google for quick access without remembering passwords.
- You can switch accounts anytime via the settings screen.

---

## 🏁 What's Next?

Once logged in, you’ll be redirected to the **main menu**, where you can:
- Choose between memory, logic, and attention games
- View your progress and stats
- Access and update your user profile
