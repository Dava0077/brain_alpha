# 🆕 Sign-Up Screen - User Guide

## 🎯 Objective

The **Sign-Up Screen** allows new users to **create an account** to access the full features of the app — including tracking progress, saving game sessions, and unlocking higher difficulty levels.

Creating an account ensures your game data (like neurons and completed sessions) is stored securely and can be accessed across devices.

---

## 📝 How to Register

1. Tap on the **Create Account** button from the login screen.
2. You’ll be prompted to enter:
   - 📧 **Email Address**
   - 🔒 **Password**
   - 🧑‍💻 **Username** (optional, auto-generated if skipped)

3. Tap the **Register** button.

4. If successful:
   - You’re logged in automatically.
   - You’re redirected to the app’s main menu.

---

## ✅ Registration Requirements

- **Valid email address** (used for recovery and ID).
- **Password** of at least 6 characters.
- Usernames:
   - May contain letters, numbers, underscores.
   - Cannot be longer than 20 characters.
   - Are unique across the platform.

---

## 🚫 Common Errors

| Error Message            | What It Means                                |
|--------------------------|----------------------------------------------|
| `Email already in use`   | That email is already registered             |
| `Invalid email format`   | Check for typos in the email address         |
| `Weak password`          | Password must meet minimum length/complexity |
| `Missing fields`         | You left a required input empty              |

---

## 🔐 Account Security

- All authentication is managed securely using **Supabase Auth**.
- Passwords are hashed and not stored in plain text.
- Account creation automatically ensures your user record exists in the database.

---

## 🎨 Interface Overview

- 🧾 **Form Fields:** Email, Password, Username
- 🟢 **Register Button:** Submits your info to create the account
- 🔙 **Back to Login:** Return to the login screen

---

## 👥 After Sign-Up

Once your account is created:
- You’re automatically logged in.
- Your **neuron score starts at 0**.
- Difficulty levels unlock as you accumulate neurons.
- Your sessions and progress are saved in the cloud.

## 💡 Pro Tips

- Use a valid email so you can recover your account if needed.
- Pick a username that represents you in the leaderboard.
- You can edit your profile later in the settings menu.
